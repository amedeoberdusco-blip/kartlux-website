A Luxembourg platform for international investment structures.

Maddano S.A. operates from Luxembourg, one of Europe’s leading financial centres, providing a corporate platform for investment holding, structured finance and securitisation-related activities.

The company is designed to support transparent, efficient and documented structures for investors, entrepreneurs and institutional counterparties.

Institutional approach. Focused execution.

Holding activities

Holding and management of participations, assets and investment interests through a Luxembourg corporate structure.

Structured finance

Support for structured transactions, investment platforms and corporate finance arrangements with international counterparties.

Securitisation

Use of securitisation structures and dedicated compartments in accordance with applicable Luxembourg laws and documentation.

Segregated structures for dedicated transactions.

Maddano S.A. may establish securitisation structures connected to specific assets, portfolios, receivables or investment opportunities, subject to applicable legal, tax and regulatory requirements.

Dedicated compartments may be used to segregate assets, liabilities and risks related to individual transactions or investment portfolios.

  • Asset and liability segregation
  • Transaction-specific documentation
  • Cross-border investment structuring
  • Clear governance and reporting framework

Compliance, transparency and professional standards.

Maddano S.A. aims to operate with appropriate governance, documentation and due diligence standards, including counterparty identification, transaction review and ongoing administrative oversight.
